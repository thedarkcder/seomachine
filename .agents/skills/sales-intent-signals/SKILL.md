# Intent Signals

Use this skill to decide which accounts deserve immediate action and what message angle to use.

## Signal Taxonomy

Prioritize signals by recency, relevance, and proximity to a buying event:

| Signal | Strength | Typical action |
|---|---|---|
| Demo/pricing engagement | Very high | manual follow-up |
| Competitive evaluation | Very high | differentiated outreach |
| New executive in role | High | leadership-change outreach |
| Recent funding/acquisition | High | scaling or change narrative |
| Hiring for relevant roles | Medium-high | capacity or process pain angle |
| Relevant tech adoption/removal | Medium-high | integration or replacement angle |
| Category intent topic | Medium | problem-led outreach |
| Content engagement | Medium-low | nurture or softer outreach |

## Workflow

1. Collect signals:
   - what happened
   - when it happened
   - source
   - affected account or contact
   - why it matters for the user's offer

2. Score signal quality:
   - recency: fresh beats stale
   - relevance: tied to the problem the product solves
   - specificity: account-level beats generic industry trend
   - actionability: clear next step or buyer persona

3. Map signal to action:
   - enrich contacts
   - prioritize in batch prospecting
   - personalize outreach
   - build a signal-specific cadence
   - defer to nurture

4. Messaging rule:
   - use signals to inform timing and angle
   - avoid wording that feels invasive, such as directly naming private intent data

## Output

Create a signal assessment table:

| account | signal | date | strength | likely pain | recommended angle | next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
