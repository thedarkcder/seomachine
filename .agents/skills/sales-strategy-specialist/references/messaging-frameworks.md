# Messaging Frameworks

## Trigger-Based
Use a recent company event as the opener.

## Problem-Led
Lead with a specific business pain.

## Insight-Led
Teach something useful or reframe the problem.

## Social Proof-Led
Reference relevant peer outcomes.

## Role-Based
Use role-specific relevance when personalization is limited.

## Referral/Warm Intro
Use when a genuine warm path exists.
