# Contact Search Patterns

Use searches like:

- "[company name]" CEO founder
- "[company name]" "Head of Digital"
- "[company name]" "Commercial Director"
- "[company name]" "Finance Director"
- "[company name]" podcast interview
- "[company name]" webinar speaker
- "[person name]" "[company name]"
- site:linkedin.com/in "[company name]" "[role]"
- site:youtube.com "[company name]" "[person name]"
