# Buying Roles

## Economic Buyer
Owns budget, final approval, or commercial outcome.

## Champion
Feels the pain and may support change internally.

## Technical Evaluator
Checks feasibility, integration, security, data, or implementation effort.

## User Buyer
Represents day-to-day team needs.

## Blocker
Can slow or stop the deal: legal, procurement, compliance, finance, security.

## Influencer
Does not own the decision but can shape perception.
