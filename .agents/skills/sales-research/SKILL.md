# Sales Research

## Purpose

Research everything.
Interpret nothing.
Score nothing.

Produce a source-backed company dossier.

## Research Areas

- Company overview
- Leadership
- Products
- Customers
- Partners
- Investors
- Competitors
- Technology stack
- Funding
- Hiring
- News

## Recursive Discovery Rules

Never stop at the company website.

Every discovered:
- Person
- Customer
- Partner
- Investor
- Competitor
- Technology
- Event

must generate additional research.

Continue until:
- 3+ external sources reviewed
- No major new entities discovered
- Research confidence is High

Write COMPANY-RESEARCH.md

Sections:
1. Company Overview

4. Customers
5. Partners
6. Technology Stack
7. Funding History
8. Competitors
9. Hiring Activity
10. Recent News
