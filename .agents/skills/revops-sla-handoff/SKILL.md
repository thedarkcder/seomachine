# SLA And Handoff Design

Use this skill when leads, opportunities, customers, or expansion signals need clear ownership and response expectations.

## Workflow

1. Identify handoff points:
   - marketing to SDR
   - SDR to AE
   - AE to sales engineering
   - AE to customer success
   - customer success to expansion owner
   - support or product signal to sales

2. Define the handoff package:
   - required fields
   - qualification notes
   - pain points
   - source and campaign
   - buying committee
   - next action
   - meeting or reply context

3. Define SLA by event:
   - demo request
   - high-intent lead
   - content lead
   - outbound positive reply
   - referred contact
   - closed-won customer handoff
   - renewal or expansion signal

4. Define failure handling:
   - missed SLA
   - incomplete handoff
   - rejected lead
   - duplicate owner conflict
   - customer risk handoff

## Output

Create an SLA and handoff table:

| handoff | trigger | from | to | required context | SLA | fallback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Add reason codes for accepted, rejected, recycled, and disqualified handoffs.
