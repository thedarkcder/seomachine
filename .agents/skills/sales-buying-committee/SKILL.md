# Buying Committee Mapping

Use this skill when a single contact is not enough and the user needs a multi-threaded account plan.

## Roles

- Economic buyer: owns budget or final approval
- Champion: feels the pain and can advocate internally
- Technical evaluator: checks feasibility, integration, security, or operations
- User buyer: team that uses the product day to day
- Blocker: person or team likely to resist change
- Procurement/legal/security: late-stage approval stakeholders

## Workflow

1. Identify likely roles from company type, product category, and deal size.
2. Map known contacts to roles.
3. Identify missing roles and titles to source.
4. Recommend outreach order:
   - start with champion for pain discovery
   - involve economic buyer once pain and value are clear
   - prepare evaluator-specific proof for technical stakeholders
5. Create role-specific messaging angles.

## Output

Create a buying committee map:

| role | likely titles | known contact | confidence | message angle | next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

If the account is SMB or owner-operated, state when a smaller committee is likely.
