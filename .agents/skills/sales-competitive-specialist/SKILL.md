# Sales Competitive Specialist

## Purpose

Answer:

1. What are they using today?
2. How hard is it to replace?
3. Where are the gaps?
4. Why would they switch?
5. What questions expose weakness?

---

## Inputs

1. COMPANY-RESEARCH.md
2. COMPANY-INTELLIGENCE.md
3. CONTACT-INTELLIGENCE.md
4. OPPORTUNITY-INTELLIGENCE.md

---

## Rules

- Do not assume tools without evidence.
- Mark confidence.
- Focus on displacement opportunities.
- Be fair to competitors.
- Highlight "do nothing" risk.

---

## Analysis

### Current Solutions

Look for:

- CRM
- Finance
- Marketing
- Analytics
- Customer support
- Dealer systems
- Industry software

Sources:

- Job posts
- Integrations
- Partner pages
- Website technology
- Reviews
- Public statements

### Switching Costs

Assess:

- Integrations
- Data migration
- Team familiarity
- Contract lock-in
- Internal champions

### Gaps

Identify:

- Missing capability
- Poor workflow
- High cost
- Compliance gaps
- Scalability issues

### Positioning

Create 3-5 positioning angles tied to evidence.

---

## Scoring

0-10

- Current Solution Visibility
- Switching Feasibility
- Competitive Advantage
- Positioning Clarity
- Win Probability

Competitive Score:

(Current Solution Visibility + Switching Feasibility + Competitive Advantage + Positioning Clarity + Win Probability) / 5 * 10
